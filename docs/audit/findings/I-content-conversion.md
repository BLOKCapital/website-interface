## 3. Extra sections

### 3.1 Proposed event taxonomy (minimal, consent-respecting)

**Rules.** Nothing fires unless `hasConsent("analytics")` is true and `window.gtag` exists (keep today's "basic" Consent Mode, with no cookieless pings to Google). Parameter values are lowercase snake_case enums with no addresses, emails, free text or IDs. Disable Google signals and ad personalisation in the GA4 property, which keeps the banner's promise. Register `placement`, `cta_id`, `destination`, `content_type`, `content_id` and `section_id` as event-scoped custom dimensions.

**Implementation sketch (owner/engineering).** Add `src/lib/analytics.ts` exporting `track(name, params)`, which does nothing without consent. In `Analytics.tsx`, attach one delegated `click` listener for `a[data-cta]` that reads `data-cta` and `data-placement`. Server components then only need data attributes, and client islands call `track()` directly. GA4 sends with `sendBeacon`, so new-tab outbound clicks are kept.

**Consent choice rates (counts only).** GA4 can't see people who reject, by design, and that's correct. Never send a rejection to Google. Options, for owner and legal to choose:

3. **Self-hosted counter:** a Pages Function that increments `accept | reject | custom | gpc` totals and stores no IP, user agent, timestamp-per-user or ID. This is arguably consent-accountability data, but legal must confirm.
