## 1. Scope & method

- Playwright 1.64 with Google Chrome (`isMobile`, `hasTouch`, DPR 2) against the static baseline export. While :4173 was down (EMFILE crash), I used :4191, which serves the same `baseline-src/out`. The key results were re-run on :4173 after the restart. No WebKit was available, so iOS Safari-specific behaviour is inferred from code and marked **unverified**.
- **Viewports:** 320×568, 360×740, 375×667, 375×812, 390×844, 414×896, 667×375, 844×390, 768×1024, 820×1180, 1024×768 and 1024×1366. I checked the first screen with the consent banner, header heights at the top of the page and after scrolling, the mobile menu (geometry, scroll lock, same-page links, rotation), and the cookie dialog.
- **Sweeps on 10 routes at 320/375/768/1024:** horizontal scroll regions, truncated or clipped text, orphaned grid items, and a touch-target census at 375 (WCAG 2.5.8, including the spacing exception, plus the 44 px target).
- **Explainers on touch at 375:** panel height per state, whether autoplay continues after a tap, the Hint tooltip on tap, orbit target sizes, and the range input.
- **Performance feel:** a programmatic scroll of `/` and `/indices` at 4× CPU throttle showed p95 frame time 17 ms and 0 long tasks after hydration. The menu opened 680 ms after navigation commit with 4× CPU on localhost. I saw no scroll jank. The metrics belong to E.
- Scripts and screenshots: `scratchpad/tools/agent-G/` (`firstscreen`, `menu`, `targets`, `spacing`, `layout`, `elshots`, `explainers`, `hint`, `dialog`, `samepage`, `jank`, `earlytap` `.mjs`; images in `shots/`).

## 3. Recommended first-screen arrangement (phones)

Budget for **375×667** (iPhone SE, and in-app browsers on larger phones): about 64 px of header, at most 130 px of consent card, and the h1, a 4–6 line lead and the primary CTA above the card.

1. **Header (< 640 px):** logo, an icon-only Discord button (44 px) and the menu button (44 px). **Drop the announcement bar below 640 px.** Its CTA moves into the header, which saves 40 px and also fixes MOB-02. Keep the bar at 640 px and above.
2. **Hero:** `pt-32` → `pt-24` on phones (header 64 + 32), which saves about 32 px. The three "facts" can stay below the CTAs. Lead length is copy (I).
3. **Consent card (phones, portrait):** no heading, or the heading inline in bold. One sentence plus the policy link. **Reject all, Accept all and Manage in one row** (Manage as a ghost button of equal height). That is about 120 px instead of 190–241. Keep equal prominence and the 600 ms delay (H to confirm).
4. **Landscape and short screens (`max-height: 500px`):** the consent becomes a full-width bottom bar of about 64 px (text on the left, buttons on the right). No announcement bar. The header is 52 px and hides on scroll-down. The hero visual is hidden, or placed beside the text so the h1 and CTA fit in 375 px.
5. **Projected at 375×667:** chrome about 184 px (28%) instead of 307 px (46%). The "Explore the indices" CTA ends at about 498 px, above the banner top at about 547 px. At 320×568 the lead becomes readable. In landscape about 60% of the screen is usable instead of 20%.

## 4. Keep

- **No horizontal page overflow** from 320 to 1920. Phone strips (`ProtocolExplorer`, `RulesDemo`) show a peek of the next tab and scroll only horizontally, so the page never jumps.
- **No scroll-jacking anywhere.** `HowItWorksScroller` drops its sticky mock below `lg`, and `ProductStack`'s sticky deck is ≥ 1024 only and uses `svh`.
- `RulesDemo` stops autoplay on the first tap. Copy this pattern into MOB-04 and MOB-11.
- The **Hint bubble becomes a fixed bottom sheet on phones**: `display:none` while hidden, so there is no overflow. It worked on tap in Chromium and closed on tapping elsewhere.
- Button `md`/`lg` sizes (44 and 48 px), mobile menu rows (about 60 px), FAQ `<details>` summaries (64 px or more), footer links (34 px), social icons (40 px with an 8 px gap). **No WCAG 2.5.8 failures found.**
- Mobile menu: `inert` while closed, Escape closes it and returns focus, scroll is locked while open, and the circle reveal is shortened under reduced motion.
- Touch-equivalent paths exist for pointer explainers: `AllocationExplorer` responds to `pointerenter` on tap and has a list of `aria-pressed` buttons, and `BasketOrbit` (37 px tokens at 375) is mirrored by the component list.
- The type scale clamps well at 320: h1 40 px in 2–3 lines, h2 30 px, footer display 40 px. There is no oversized display type.
- The cookie dialog is a bottom sheet on phones, Reject and Accept are equally prominent, and inputs are 16 px (no iOS focus zoom).

## 5. Cross-agent notes

- **A (UX/IA):** the hero "How it works" button links to `#explore` ("Explore the protocol"), not the `#how` section titled "How it works" (`Hero.tsx:90`). The page-length candidates are in MOB-12.
- **D (a11y):** while the mobile menu is open, `main` and `footer` are not inert, so keyboard focus can leave the sheet into the page behind it (code read, `Nav.tsx`, not keyboard-tested). There are pause-mechanism questions for `DecisionPipeline` and `ProtocolExplorer` under 2.2.2. The phone tab strips declare `aria-orientation="vertical"` while laid out horizontally (`ProtocolExplorer.tsx:106`, `RulesDemo.tsx:126`).
- **E (perf):** `useOverDarkBand` runs `querySelectorAll` plus `getBoundingClientRect` on every dark band on every scroll event (`hooks.ts:48-65`). The announcement bar animates `grid-template-rows` on the scroll threshold. Many infinite animations run on phones (hero float and flow lines, orbit, scanlines, GlyphField), which costs battery.
- **C (motion):** needs a spec for header hide-on-scroll in short viewports (MOB-13). The ProtocolExplorer height animation should be CSS-only.
- **H (consent):** the compact phone and landscape banner layout in §3 needs your sign-off on equal prominence and wording. The `env(safe-area-*)` padding in the banner is inert (MOB-22).
- **I (copy):** "Click a token to inspect it" should say tap/select (`IndexExplorer.tsx:116`). The "Press ⌘C" fallback (MOB-23). A shorter announcement CTA is needed under 360 px.
- **B (visual):** Ecosystem tiles are cramped at 1024 (4 columns in 8/12). The audits grid at 768 (MOB-09).
- **K / SITE_MAP:** SITE_MAP §1 lists the **footer** as a Night-garden (`.theme-dark`) component, but `Footer.tsx:55` has no `theme-dark` and renders light (`shots/el_footer_375.jpg`). Either the docs or the code is stale.
