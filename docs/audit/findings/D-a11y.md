# D · Accessibility (WCAG 2.2 AA)

Auditor: Agent D · `redesign` @ `5beb0ee` · 2026-10-11 · ID prefix `A11Y-`

## 1. Scope & method

- **Code review** of every interactive island and shared primitive: nav (`DesktopNav`, `MobileMenu`, `AnnouncementBar`), `CookieConsent`, the tablists/explorers, `RebalanceLab`, `AllocationExplorer`, `DecisionPipeline`, `ProposalBoard`, `FaqList`, `CopyButton`, `Hint`, `PolicyTOC`, `LiveStatus`, `ProductVideo`, the motion text (`SplitText`, `Scramble`, `CountUp`, `ScrollInk`), canvases and `globals.css` focus and motion rules.
- **Playwright (Chrome, headless)** against the static export. While :4173 was down (EMFILE crash, about 12:40) I served the same `baseline-src/out` on a private port (:4191, now stopped). Later checks ran on the restarted :4173. Scripts and screenshots are in `scratchpad/tools/agent-D/` (`tabwalk`, `tabwalk-rev`, `widgets-desktop`, `widgets-mobile`, `targets`, `misc`, `tabs`, `axe-states`, `contrast`, `spacing`, `bar`, `outline`; images in `shots/`).
- **Keyboard:** I walked full Tab sequences on `/`, `/indices` and `/token` at 1280 and 375, plus Shift+Tab on `/` and walks with the consent banner showing. Each stop recorded the element, its box, outline style, ring contrast against the backdrop, clipping by `overflow` ancestors, and how much of the element was covered (9-point `elementFromPoint` sample). I also ran scripted checks of the skip link, desktop menus, mobile sheet, consent banner and dialog, tabs, Hint, CopyButton, video facade and client-side navigation focus.
- **Semantics:** I extracted landmarks, the h1–h6 outline and CDP accessibility-tree names for 11 routes at both widths. I ran axe (same tags as the baseline) on states the baseline never opened: banner, cookie dialog, mobile sheet, each desktop menu panel and the header over a dark band.
- **Contrast:** axe, plus computed ratios for focus rings, badges, switches, tab states and chart segments. I measured target sizes at 375 on 10 routes with the 2.5.8 spacing test, ran a 1.4.12 text-spacing pass, watched live regions for 80 s, and checked the YouTube caption tracks.

**Result in one line:** the base is good (a real focus ring, labelled landmarks, sr-only transcripts, thorough reduced-motion support). The serious problems are in the shell. **Both navigation menus break focus order (F85)**, the consent banner hides focused elements and sits last in the tab order, and the primary Discord CTA in the announcement bar has an invisible focus ring.
