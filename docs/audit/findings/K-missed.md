## 1. Scope & method

- **Dev-mode hydration and console:** I ran `next dev` on :4180 in a private clone of `baseline-src` (`tools/agent-K/dev`, stopped afterwards). Playwright swept all 15 routes at 1280 with a full scroll-through, three times: (a) default timezone and locale, (b) `Pacific/Kiritimati` (UTC+14) with `de-DE` and a dismissed announcement bar, (c) 375 mobile plus 1280 with "Accept all", then client-side navigation through 10 routes, back/forward and an anchor jump.
- **Links:** I crawled every `<a>`, `src` and `srcset` in the 15 exported HTML files (`baseline-src/out`) and checked that each internal target and `#anchor` exists. I then sent one GET (manual redirects, 15 s timeout, Chrome UA, 4 at a time) to each of 64 unique external URLs plus the URLs in llms.txt and the sitemap. I also queried the Discord invite API, YouTube oEmbed and the live site's legacy paths.
- **Print:** `page.pdf` (A4, backgrounds on and off) for the user agreement (banner still showing), the cookie policy, `/token`, `/` and `/indices/blokc5`, served from my own static server. I read the token and user-agreement PDFs page by page.
- **Code and asset review:** icons, manifest, OG, JSON-LD logo (decoded PNG pixels for colours and the maskable safe zone), compiled CSS (prefixes, `@media print`, classes that compile to nothing), a browserslist resolution, `npm outdated` and `npm audit` (full and `--omit=dev`), `tsc --noUnusedLocals`, eslint, an import and export graph, public-asset references, data dates and brand strings.
- **Not done:** no WebKit or Firefox runs (not installed, so compatibility is reasoned from features). DocSend, Figma, Arbiscan address pages and LinkedIn block headless or bot clients, so those links are **unverified**.
